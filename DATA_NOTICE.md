# Data and rights notice

This repository mixes original analysis/code with material derived from public social-media and benchmark sources.

## Human-study data

The Beat the Robot release is deidentified. It omits participant nicknames, original session/participant UUIDs, and exact public-game timestamps. The released records are intended for research verification of the paper's analyses.

## Public social-media material

Bluesky/Twitter-derived files may contain public post text, public post identifiers, handles, destination URLs, or derived labels. Copyright and platform rights in those source posts remain with their respective rightsholders. Inclusion here does not purport to relicense third-party content.

## Benchmark provenance

The historical task derives from Tan, Lee, and Pang (ACL 2014) and the public HypoBench retweet split. See the paper references and the component documentation for provenance.
